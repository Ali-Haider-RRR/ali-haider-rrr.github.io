Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

LydraCare ("we", "us") provides a mobile app that helps seniors, their caregivers and their family members manage medications, appointments, daily check-ins and related care information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@lydracare.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed), language, time zone, and profile photo if you add one. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier and email.
  • Health and care information you enter: medications and dose times, whether doses were taken or skipped, appointments (including the place you choose), daily check-ins (mood and pain), exercise logs, reminders, and emergency contacts.
  • Family and caregiver links: which people are connected as a caregiver, senior or family member, and the permissions you give them.
  • Location: only when you choose "use my current location" or pick a place for an appointment. Saved places are stored encrypted.
  • Device information: a push-notification token so we can send you alerts and reminders.
  • Subscriptions: purchase status from Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your card details.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your medications, appointments and reminders, and keep your family circle in sync.
  • To send notifications and emails you ask for: login and password codes, reminders, missed-dose and appointment alerts to your caregivers, and an optional weekly report (off by default).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

Who can see your information

Only the people you connect with in the app, according to the permissions you set (for example a caregiver or family member in your circle). We do not share your data with anyone else except the service providers below, who process it for us.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database hosting.
  • Hostinger — server hosting and email mailboxes.
  • Resend — sending emails (codes, reports).
  • Cloudinary — storing profile photos.
  • Expo — delivering push notifications.
  • Google — sign-in, and place search (requests go through our server).
  • Google Play and Apple — subscriptions and app distribution.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our systems, except where the law requires us to keep something.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, go to Settings and choose delete account. You can also email support@lydracare.com from the address on your account and we will delete it.
  • Access or correct your data: you can edit most information in the app, or ask us by email.
  • Notifications and emails: turn them off in your phone settings or in the app. The weekly report can be switched off in Settings.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Access to servers is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

LydraCare is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Not medical advice

LydraCare is a reminder and organising tool. It does not give medical advice and is not a substitute for a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
